## Design direction and implementation

The approved Inter, Signal blue, surface tokens and exact logo are retained. Stronger composition comes from bounded editorial type, short chapter headers, purposeful imagery, exact-model hardware, a guided operational sequence and tighter desktop rhythm. No animation library, WebGL, generated imagery or new runtime dependency was added. Inter is self-hosted under the included OFL; this removes a build-time Google Fonts dependency while preserving the typeface.

- Homepage rebuilt as five compact sections. Principal demo opens from the hero in a native modal. WIN / HIRE / RUN / EQUIP / PROVE become one keyboard-accessible chapter selector rather than five repeated narratives.
- GUARD platform rebuilt around the operational sequence, working capabilities, scoped access and assignment safeguards. Patrol uses the same demonstration. Attendance has an editorial allocation → clock event → assessment plate.
- Equipment has an exact-model gallery, category filters, touch-safe manual model controls and model-specific quote links. The selected model is validated and prefilled in the existing intake form. Unphotographed exact models remain textual; no stand-ins.
- Shared PageHero and SplitHero now establish tighter, bounded type and consistent rhythm. Marketplace loses its invented profile interface and explicitly describes planned functionality. Guards, sales recruitment, pricing, contact and profile/onboarding journeys get the same scale, surfaces and conversion treatment.
- Existing forms, analytics event names, pricing module, canonical metadata, redirects and env-driven acquisition links retained. ProductProof now safely returns nothing when no approved captures exist.

## Independent design critique

Reviewed full desktop and mobile homepage and product screenshots after implementation, separately from the test results. The homepage is substantially shorter; hardware has useful physical scale, exact imagery and a strong commercial CTA; the product story is interactive and explicitly labelled. Mobile controls remain usable rather than relying on hover. Initial review caught an off-centre modal, missing loading fallback, unsupported diagonal arrow glyphs and weak onboarding hierarchy; those were corrected.

Remaining weaknesses: the first screen still relies on the existing illustrated operation; authentic software remains visually unavailable; no claim of exceeding ATEX can be substantiated while the benchmark is inaccessible. These are material design acceptance gaps, not reasons to invent assets. Verdict and exact verification results are in `docs/qa/mission/REPORT.md`.
