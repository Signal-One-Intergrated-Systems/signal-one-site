# Signal One · website-wide optical glass + photographic scene mission

Status: in progress. Delivery is incremental; no claim that all pages are complete.

## Fixed design laws
1. Original Signal One vector logo is immutable; avoid competing tint and ensure contrast.
2. One header: outer shell transparent, inner capsule owns a neutral graphite blurred glass. Never put it on another blue panel.
3. Human-first documentary photography is primary on every marketing hero and showcase. Premium architectural scenes remain secondary background context behind the subject and copy; distinct photographs are preferred where a suitable local asset exists.
4. People demonstrate the offering: Guard = professional officers, supervisors and control-room staff at work; Service = real-looking, diverse hospitality teams engaged in service; Equipment = deployed professionals with approved genuine product photos. Unoccupied spaces may only serve as background context, not replace people in the featured media.
5. Hero globe stays behind the left information card, never over the security staff. Preserve actual Canvas land/city path animation; avoid CSS blur and aggressive scaling.
6. Keep product status claims accurate; 14-day trials are requests until tenant provisioning is verified.
7. Clickable platform wordmarks 1GUARD, 1Service, 1Community must go to a meaningful route/anchor; 1Community is coming soon and cannot be described as launched.
8. Shared motion: subtle hover, tactile click, modest on-enter movement; no scroll-jacking, no needless animation, protect reduced-motion and reduced-transparency users.
9. No generic fake hardware, fake product screens, fake proof badges, testimonials or financial claims.
10. Keep logo, shared tokens, accessible focus, touch targets and truthful pricing unchanged.
