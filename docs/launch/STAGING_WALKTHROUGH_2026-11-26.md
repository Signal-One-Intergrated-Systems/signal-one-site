# Staging walkthrough — 2026-11-26

Independent reviewer pass over the rendered public website on staging (`https://web-staging-review-production.up.railway.app`). Nothing was fixed. Production was not loaded and no production form was submitted.

**Method.** Playwright (Chromium), fresh context, no cookies. 22 routes (20 from `sitemap.xml`, plus `/join/sales` from the navigation and an unknown URL for the 404) at 1440×900, 1280×800, 1024×768 and 390×844 (mobile, touch). Each page was captured as scrolled viewport frames, stitched into a full page, and **viewed**. Interactions: header and mobile menu (open, focus trap, Escape, link taps), the four communications journeys from the homepage and from `/radios-equipment`, every product "Request a quote" deep link, every "What do you need?" option and all three quote categories, empty-submit validation, the error summary and focus, valid submits of every form (quote, contact, trial request, guard join, sales application, Guard Marketplace interest), keyboard-only use of `/contact`, reduced motion, the 404, every internal link, mailto and WhatsApp hrefs, and axe WCAG 2.1 AA on all routes at 1440 and 390.

**Screenshot folder:** `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/880ad5ad-058b-5c40-9bba-4c19903c0d78/scratchpad/walkthrough/`. Full pages are named `<width>_<route>.jpg`, for example `1440_radios-equipment.jpg`. The folder is session scratch space, so copy anything you want to keep.

## Verdict: **Ready with fixes**

The site is bright, honest and technically clean. There are no console errors, no broken links or images, no horizontal overflow at any width, no axe violations, and the old number and registered company name appear nowhere. Every form says requests are handled by email, prepares an email and never claims an enquiry was received. One S1 layout break on the main commercial page must be fixed before release. Seven S2 items should be fixed before release.

## Passed checks

- **Contact details:** every page uses WhatsApp `+27 77 484 4280` (`https://wa.me/27774844280`), `sales@signalone.co.za` and `support@signalone.co.za`. **PASS.** The old number `+27 60 633 5870` appears on no page. **PASS.** The registered company name and "(Pty)" appear on no public page. **PASS.**
- **Form honesty:** every form carries "Requests are currently handled by email … Your request is only sent when you send that email". On staging the submit POSTs to `/api/intake`, receives 503, and the page then says "nothing was sent" and offers the prepared email. No form ever claimed receipt. **PASS** (presentation: SW-08).
- **Journeys:** all four homepage and `/radios-equipment` journeys preselect the right "What do you need?" value. Tracking and body-camera deep links switch category and preselect FMC920 or FMB920. **PASS** except E600 (SW-03).
- **Validation:** empty submit gives a linked error summary ("There are 10 problems"), focus moves to the summary, each field gets `aria-invalid`, and the summary links focus the field. A keyboard-only run of `/contact` works end to end with visible focus rings. **PASS.**
- **Mobile menu:** `aria-expanded` toggles, focus is trapped, Escape closes and returns focus to "Menu", the body scroll locks, and links navigate and close the menu. **PASS.**
- **Truthfulness:** no free or instant claims, no invented prices beyond the R2,50 guard-day rate, equipment "By quote" everywhere, "renting a device does not yet connect it to the platform" on `/radios-equipment` and `/ptt-radio-rental`, and 1Service "In development" with a disclaimer under the capabilities. **PASS** except SW-05, SW-06 and SW-21.
- **Technical:** 0 console errors and 0 failed requests on 21 routes × 4 widths. The 404 returns status 404. All 36 internal links return 200, and the Hytera manufacturer links resolve. No horizontal scroll at any width. The header stays one line (80 px) at 1280 and 1440. axe WCAG 2.1 AA reports 0 violations at 1440 and 390. Reduced motion: 0 animated elements and all content visible.

## What works well

- It is bright and light-first. White and off-white surfaces, dark readable type, Signal One blue accents, graphite kept to the 1GUARD band, the coverage section and the footer.
- The honesty is unusually good: "What it does not do yet" blocks, "What Signal One does not do" on PSiRA, "Illustrative scene · fictional security company" captions, and "Nothing is supplied until you accept the quote".
- `/pricing` is excellent for a buyer: one rate, the guard-day definition, the rules in full, worked examples, and a calculator with VAT shown.
- The equipment catalogue (where it renders) gives real spec tables with manufacturer source links, which is exactly what procurement needs.
- Form craft is high: conditional fields per need, GOV.UK-style error summaries, progress steps, review and edit screens, and drafts saved on the phone for guards.
- `/support` is clear and safe: an emergency disclaimer, subject-prefilled mailto per issue type, and a "what to include" list.
