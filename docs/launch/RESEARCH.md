# Signal One: website research and requirements

Prepared 2026-11-25. Desk research only. No repository was modified and no form was submitted anywhere.

## 0. How the evidence was gathered (read first)

- **Network limit:** This session's network allowlist blocked direct page loads from nngroup.com and baymard.com (DNS refused). Because the block is at the allowlist level, w3.org, web.dev, gov.uk, gartner.com and the benchmark brands' sites were not fetched either; nothing was guessed to fill the gap.
- **How those pages were checked instead:** Their content was verified through web-search excerpts of the named pages. A citation marked **[S]** below means "page content seen as a search excerpt, not loaded in full". A finding is not stated where the excerpt did not confirm it; for example, NN/g's "a single evaluator finds 35%" figure could not be confirmed and is not used.
- **Pages loaded directly [F]:** Hytera PoC overview https://www.hytera.com/en/product-new/lte-broadband/poc-radios.html, Hytera PNC360S https://www.hytera.com/en/product-new/lte-broadband/poc-radio/pnc360s.html, and the Hytera iMvula case study (URL in Part B).
- **Re-checking the [S] rows:** To re-verify them by direct fetch, the session's network settings need these hosts allowed: www.nngroup.com, baymard.com, www.w3.org, web.dev, www.gov.uk, www.gartner.com, and the benchmark brand sites.
- **Evidence labels:**
  - **Research-supported:** an empirical study or survey with a stated method.
  - **Practitioner guidance:** expert heuristics, standards, platform documentation, or qualitative consultancy findings without published effect sizes.
  - **Hypothesis:** a plausible inference for Signal One that has not been tested.
  - WCAG and POPIA are standards or law. They are marked **Standard**, which means the criterion's text is verified, not that the design effect has been proven.
- **Facts about Signal One:** Only the observed facts supplied in the brief are used: mailto-only forms, radios missing from the nav, an abstract hero, equipment about 8 screens down, spec-less cards, quoted-not-listed prices with 12/24/36-month terms, email-only contact, "coming soon" language, inconsistent naming of 1GUARD, mobile homepage lab LCP of 4.4 s, and landing pages at about 1.0 s.

### A9. Anti-patterns to avoid

| Anti-pattern | Evidence | Rule for Signal One |
|---|---|---|
| Fake scarcity ("Only 3 left", fake countdowns) | Mathur et al. (CSCW 2019) crawled about 11K shopping sites. They found 1,818 dark-pattern instances on 1,254 sites, deceptive low-stock messages on 17 sites, and 183 sites with outright deception. https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.07032 [S] | No stock or urgency claims unless they come from live inventory. No countdown timers on offers. |
| Fake social proof (invented "X just requested a quote", reused testimonials) | The same study found 313 activity messages on 264 sites, and 12 reused-testimonial instances, one set reappearing under different names. [S] | Testimonials are real, attributable (name, role, company, with permission) and dated. No activity pop-ups. |
| Inflated or inconsistent counts ("Trusted by 500+ companies") | Verkada's own pages show 30,000+ on some and 33,500+ on others (https://verkada.com/try [S]). Inconsistency undermines credibility even at large firms. | Publish a number only if it can be shown; keep it in one place and update it there. |
| Hidden or drip pricing | NN/g: surprise fees damage loyalty (https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ecommerce-taxes-fees/ [S]). The FTC 2022 report covers designs that "hide or delay" information. https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P214800%20Dark%20Patterns%20Report%209.14.2022%20-%20FINAL.pdf [S] | All recurring costs are disclosed before the quote request (A3.7). |
| Pre-ticked consent, or combined "quote + marketing" consent | FTC 2022 report on designs that subvert privacy choices [S]; POPIA (A8). | Opt-in is separate and unticked. |
| Implied manufacturer endorsement | Hytera runs a "Find a Dealer" and certification lookup [F], so buyers can check partner status. | Claim "authorised dealer/partner" only with written confirmation; otherwise say "we supply Hytera devices". |
| Overstated coverage ("works everywhere in SA") | Hytera says PoC runs "over existing cellular and WLAN networks" [S]. Motorola says SIM-free use "depends on geographic availability and network compatibility" [S]. | Say "works wherever the selected mobile network has coverage", and offer a coverage check by site address. |
| Loud "trust us" copy | A DHL e-commerce guide says explicit claims of trust or honesty "sow seeds of doubt" (vendor guidance). https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-my/e-commerce/e-commerce-advice/help-consumers-trust-your-brand [S] | Show proof (registration number, address, real customers, terms) instead of adjectives. |

### B4. Honest ways to stand out (claim only what is true today)

1. **One supplier for a security company's field stack.** PoC radios, vehicle trackers, body cameras and 1GUARD come on one account, one invoice and one support line. None of the radio sellers we saw offer guard-management software. This only works if the bundle is orderable today.
2. **Published all-in rental pricing for PoC radios.** We found no SA seller publishing a PoC *rental* price (B2: Satlec publishes purchase prices only; Radio Trunk publishes no rates). A "from R__ per radio per month, SIM, data and platform included, 24 months" figure would be distinctive.
3. **Published per-guard software pricing in rand.** 1GUARD's R2,50 per guard per day (about R75 a month for 30 days) is transparent. Competitors on Capterra quote mostly in USD or GBP. Show the monthly equivalent and what's included.
4. **Risk-reducing first order.** A coverage check by site address, plus a short pilot and a named account contact, answers the "new supplier" risk (A4.8). Verkada and Samsara use trials; we saw no SA radio seller doing so.
5. **Fast local response, stated and measured.** WhatsApp and phone with a published reply time (e.g. a quote in 1 business day). It must be staffed and tracked before it is advertised.

10. Validate through a 3–5-evaluator heuristic review plus moderated rounds of 5–6 target users (A7).
