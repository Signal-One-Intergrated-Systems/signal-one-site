# Signal One public website: image inventory (Workstream A2)

**Subject:** live https://signalone.co.za (`origin/main` @ `8e89d24`), captured 2026-11-25 at 1440×900 and 390×844 (plus 1280 and 768 for `/`, radios, rental, 1GUARD). Companion to `BRAND_AUDIT.md` (findings VA-14 … VA-20).

## 1. Method

- Every `<img>` and every computed CSS `background-image` photograph on all 20 routes was recorded from the rendered DOM (130 placements at all widths, **41 distinct route × section × asset placements at 1440/390, 23 delivered files, 19 distinct assets**). Inline SVG logos and the 20 px check icons are excluded (covered in BRAND_AUDIT §3.5).
- Each delivered file was downloaded from live and measured: source pixels, mean sRGB, luma, mean HSV saturation, R−B warmth, McCamy CCT estimate from the mean colour, and for products the alpha bounding box (device occupancy of the canvas).
- Scale: "scale at 1×" is drawn-pixels ÷ source-pixels for the fit in use (`contain` = min, `cover` = max); > 1.00 means the raster is **upscaled** at DPR 1. "Retina headroom" = source ÷ (2 × drawn); < 1.00 means the image is soft on 2× screens. Next/Image's density-corrected `naturalWidth` was not used for this, because it under-reports the decoded bitmap.
- Hashes of the locked hero (`4d3aca7b…`), the control room (`b8efd081…`) and the SC780/P30 Lite originals were verified against `docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` — all match.
- Product accuracy: compared pixel-for-pixel / visually with masters fetched from www.hytera.com (PNC360S, P30, SC780 product pages) and wiki.teltonika-gps.com (FMB920, FMC920). www.teltonika-gps.com redirects were refused by this sandbox; hytera.co.za / hytera.ae (the documented P30 Lite and SC780 sources) were unreachable.
- Contact sheets: `shots/sheets/images_rendered_0..3.jpg` (every placement as rendered, cropped from the full-page captures, 82 tiles), `shots/sheets/images_source_assets.jpg` (every source asset with stats), `shots/sheets/product_vs_manufacturer.jpg`, `shots/sheets/ai_check_1.jpg` (people photographs at inspection size).
